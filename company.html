OUR COMPANY

DMSP Engineering Private Limited and DMSP Engineering LLC are associate companies providing telecom engineering and geospatial services.

Degree Minute Second Precessions (DMSP) Engineering Private Limited was established on 18 February 2024 in Noida, Delhi NCR, India. A branch office was established in Bhubaneswar and Berhampur in October 2025 to support high-volume, cost-effective quality data production.

DMSP Engineering LLC was established on 20 August 2026 in Delaware, USA, with a vision to support turnkey telecom engineering solutions in the United States.
